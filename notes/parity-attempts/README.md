# Parity-barrier attempts: route index

- 2026-11-14 — [Asymptotic sieve Type II hypothesis for twin primes](2026-11-14-asymptotic-sieve-type-ii-for-twin-primes.md) — **conditional** — Friedlander–Iwaniec Thm 2 would give the twin-prime asymptotic from (R₃) at level > x^{2/3} plus (B) for Λ(n+2); (B) is exactly a bilinear λ(k)Λ(k+2) estimate, fails identically for the parity-twisted sequence, and is documented as out of reach (FI98 p. 1046; Polymath8b §8).
- 2026-11-15 — [Tao's two-point theorem along sieve weights: the twisted Type I input of the obstruction](2026-11-15-tao-twisted-typeI-weights.md) — **proved (restricted regime)** — bounded-level, log-averaged weights cannot tell λ(n)λ(n+2)=+1 from −1 (Prop. prop:tao-weight); uniformity in the modulus up to x^θ is open, and would only confirm the obstruction.
- 2026-11-15 — [Which close-prime targets the PM8b obstruction blocks: a bipartite criterion](2026-11-15-bipartite-criterion.md) — **proved (for the stated weight class)** — a fair Liouville-pattern weight blocks a target iff (1/2,...,1/2) is in the hull of target-free sets: graphs iff bipartite, DHL[k,m+1] iff k <= 2m, windows iff no 2m+1 tuple elements in the window; so the class forbids exactly H_m < H(2m+1) (= 6 for m = 1); PM8b Remark 8.1's printed weight is identically 0 (typo-level; corrected version works).
- 2026-11-15 — [Tao–Teräväinen at level (log x)^{c0}](2026-11-15-tt25-level-logc.md) (audit: [2026-11-15-tt25-audit.md](2026-11-15-tt25-audit.md)) — **proved, conditional on a preprint** — expanding in the moduli [d1,d2] ≤ R^2 (not the period) and applying arXiv:2512.01739v2 Thm 3.1 class by class gives the twisted Type I input for w_R = (Σ_{d|n(n+2), d≤R} λ_d)^2 at R ≤ (log x)^{c0}, uniformly in |λ_d| ≤ 1 (paper Prop. prop:tao-weight-logc); first unresolved implication: (3.4)-type cancellation for moduli W far beyond a power of log x; only confirms the obstruction, no lower bound for twin primes.
- 2026-11-15 (bonus run) — [The Sawin–Shusterman step with no integer analogue](2026-11-15-ss-step-over-Z.md) — **located (reformulation)** — over Z their split Λ = −μ log ∗ 1 at D gives, unconditionally (BV), twin-prime asymptotic ⇔ T_D(x) = o(x), T_D a μ–Λ correlation (Prop. prop:ss-split; compare Murty–Vatwani Thm 1.1 as quoted); in F_q[T] T_D is bounded via the characteristic-p identity (r+s^p)' = r' (SS Lemma 3.2), which has no integer analogue; first unresolved implication: T_D = o(x) for one admissible D. Also Cor. cor:maynard-logc (two-variable weights at level (log x)^{c/32}, conditional on TT25).
- 2026-11-15 (Session 21) — [Where Polymath8b's GEH criteria stop; k = 2 exactly at the parity value](2026-11-15-geh-ceilings-k2.md) — **proved (confirms and locates the obstruction)** — all three GEH criteria of PM8b have ratio <= k^2 log k/(k-1)^2 (scaling of PM8b Cor 6.4), so no DHL[k,3] for k <= 45 although parity blocks only k <= 4; at k = 2 the Thm 3.14 ratio is <= 2 with equality (variance argument using the vanishing marginal condition, paper Thm thm:k2); the criteria and the parity class agree on every blocked case; the printed proof of PM8b Prop 6.5 has a gap (fixed with a weaker constant). No parity-breaking content.
- 2026-11-15 (Session 24) — [What the level of distribution alone gives: Bombieri's asymptotic sieve](2026-11-15-bombieri-level-alone.md) — **located (literature + folklore reduction)** — under EH (Bombieri's theorem as quoted by Ford 2004) twin primes are equivalent to the (Λ⋆Λ)-weighted count of p-2 = p1p2 and nothing more (paper Prop. prop:bomb-twin); Ford: no fixed level ν < 1 suffices, nor level plus no global parity bias; Hooley's averaged condition open for α+ν > 1, and for twins it is a Möbius-on-shifted-primes statement (open even at α = 0); probable factor-2 slip in Debouzy's constant (flag). No parity-breaking content.
- 2026-11-16 (Session 25) — [The Siegel-zero world and the twisted Type I input](2026-11-16-siegel-world-typeI.md) — **located (proved under an explicit small-prime hypothesis)** — for a real character χ mod q and any ±1 completely multiplicative f, the class sums of f(n)f(n+2) mod Q = lcm(W, q) are biased by χ(b)χ(b+2)(1 − 4s₁ − …), s₁ the 1/p-sum of primes where f ≠ χ (paper Prop. prop:siegel-bias); with Tao–Teräväinen's Cor. 3.6, in a Siegel world two-point Chowla holds, but the per-modulus, unweighted form of PM8b (144) fails at the single modulus Wq unless χ has split primes of 1/p-sum ≥ 1/4 below T = q^{1/log^{1−ε}η} (Cor. cor:siegel-typeI; the averaged input is not shown to fail); first unresolved implication: whether a Siegel zero controls those small split primes. No parity-breaking content.
- 2026-11-16 (Session 26) — [Pintz's parity-sensitive dichotomy with Maynard weights](2026-11-16-pintz-dichotomy-maynard.md) — **proved (modest; possibly folklore)** — the disjunction "two primes in n+H, or a prime n+h_i with λ(n+r) = σ" needs only θM_k > 1 (bounded gaps need θM_k > 2), so it holds for admissible 5-tuples under BV (M_5 ≥ 1417255/708216, two programs); hence some even 0<|d|≤12 has λ(p+d) = −1 for infinitely many primes (Pintz 2010: 16); the target is the complete graph on H∪{r} (not blocked), while a single prescribed d is a star (blocked, Pintz's remark); first unresolved implication: one prescribed d, which the class blocks. Sidesteps, does not break, parity.
- 2026-11-16 (Session 27) — [Chen's theorem as a consistency test of the criterion](2026-11-16-chen-consistency-test.md) — **consistent (confirms the criterion; folklore)** — no non-negative weakly fair pattern weight kills "n prime, n+2 ∈ P₂" (Lean-checked), so the class does not block Chen's theorem; each half is blocked (1−ε₁ε₂ for twins, 1+ε₁ε₂ for n+2 exactly a product of two primes, Lean-checked), matching Tao 2007 and Pintz 2010 (p+2 with odd Ω infinitely often is not known); paper Remark rem:chen. No parity-breaking content.
- 2026-11-17 (Session 28) — [Gowers norms and the infinite complexity of twin primes](2026-11-17-gowers-infinite-complexity.md) — **located (proved; folklore in substance)** — random block functions show that small U^d norms of any fixed order are compatible with any shift-2 correlation and with a density-1 'fake prime' function with almost no pairs (paper Prop. prop:gowers-blind), so smallness of global Gowers norms (the arithmetic input of the Green–Tao finite-complexity theory) does not constrain twins; Tao's localisation for λ (arXiv:1605.04628 Thm 1.8: an equivalence of open conjectures; the proved k = 2 case uses MRT15) relies on multiplicativity at small primes and has no analogue for Λ at short scales; even local U^1 uniformity alone does not give the shift-2 case (f_− is a counterexample). No parity-breaking content.
- 2026-11-17 (Session 29) — [Averaging over the shift (Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Tao)](2026-11-17-mrt-averaged-hardy-littlewood.md) — **located (literature + folklore identity)** — on average over shifts the Liouville model of parity is solved for any H → ∞ (MRT15), while Λ(n)Λ(n+h) is proved for almost all h only in windows H ≥ X^{8/33+ε} (MRT17 Thm 1.3), the range below being limited by short-interval distribution of primes, not parity; the window identity Σ_x|Σ_{x<n≤x+K} f(n)|² = Σ_{|h|<K}(K−|h|)c_f(h) makes the twin count half the second difference of the second moment of primes in windows of length 3, needed to relative precision o(1/log X); no averaged statement reaches a prescribed shift (paper subsec:mrt17). First unresolved implication: averaged HL below X^{8/33}. No parity-breaking content.
- 2026-11-17 (Session 30) — [Hybrid averages and the mean prime gap (Lichtman–Teräväinen)](2026-11-17-hybrid-averages-mean-gap.md) — **located (literature + elementary lemma; folklore in substance)** — LT22 solve Σ_p μ(p+h) on average for H ≥ (log X)^{1+ε}; their μ-side input (short exponential sums on average, η ≪ H^{-δ}) is satisfied by a μ' that is +1 on every window after a prime once H^{1+δ} ≤ c log X, and their Λ-side moments fail for H = o(log X): both stop at the mean gap; the fixed-shift model and twin primes (Λ at both places) stay out.
