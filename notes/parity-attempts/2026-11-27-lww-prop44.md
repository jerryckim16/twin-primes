## Slips / terse places in LWW (none affects the result)
- **T1 (pp. 31-32, terse/notation).** The Hilbert space on {(TP, p)} and its inner product are never defined. The
  unitarity display defines it implicitly as the σ-weighted sum over the image set, and ⟨·,·⟩ is used on both
  sides. The adjoint in (4.73) must be read with respect to this inner product. On p. 36, ⟨·,·⟩_ℓ is defined
  consistently with it.
- **T2 (p. 32, remark).** "t ∈ 2Z" uses D odd. This follows from D | P_1 (same sentence) and P_1 odd, and LWW state
  D odd on p. 23 after (4.35). Not a terse place.
- **T3 (p. 32, terse).** The substitution φ = j + 1 - 2θ silently drops the phase e(-(j+1)(t' - b + a)) = 1. This
  needs t' - b + a ∈ Z, which is why t ∈ 2Z matters. The link is not said.
- **T4 (p. 33, terse).** Inserting U_T^{-1}U_T between factors, and identifying the adjoint (A_{-2}^C)* in H with the
  adjoint on C-supported functions, both use that every factor preserves C-support and that T_{-2}^C reads only
  values at Q ∈ C. Not said; filled in at Q5.
- **T5 (p. 32, notation).** Within one sentence, (u, v) denotes first a point P ∈ Ω and then a point of Ω' (where
  the second coordinate is (P_1 - P_2)/2, not P_2); (4.68) uses the second meaning. This is harmless.
- Not an LWW slip, a correction to our previous card: U2 of 2026-11-26-lww-p37-47-prop45.md says "the equality of
