# Route card: for which eps does Polymath8b's k = 3 GEH criterion exceed the parity value 2?

Date: 15 Nov 2026, Session 23 (back-to-back run 2 of 3, continued in the same conversation as Session 22 at Jerry's request).
Follows: 2026-11-15-k3-dual-orthogonal.md (Session 22) and open question 6 of the parity paper.

## Question
sigma_3(eps) = sup of the Theorem 3.14 ratio at k = 3. Polymath8b proved sigma_3(1/4) > 2 (DHL[3,2] under GEH, so H1 <= 6).
For which eps in (0, 1/2) is sigma_3(eps) > 2? Can anything rigorous be said on the upper side?

## Success / no-go criteria (fixed before computing)
- Success: a rigorous statement sigma_3(eps) < 2 for some range of eps, plus rigorous sigma_3 > 2 at more than one eps.
- No-go: every rigorous upper bound available stays above 2 for all eps.

## What was done
1. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz with fibre-length weights (the PM8b Cor 6.4 method), truncated J: gives exactly c(3) = 2.472 again
   (worst point: one coordinate near 3/2). Not useful.
2. Schur test (Prop. prop:unc-ceiling, proved here): for eps >= 1/4 the problem WITHOUT marginal condition reduces to a 1D
   inequality for phi on [0, 1-eps]; any positive phi satisfying it gives a ceiling C for every eps' >= eps. The 1D, 2D and 3D
   numerics agree (code/parity/k3_unc_1d.py, k3_unc_kernel.py, k3_thm314_dual.py 'none' mode); the unconstrained supremum
   crosses 2 at eps ~ 0.4385.
3. Certificates (two programs each): code/parity/k3_unc_cert.py (arb, adaptive boxes, monotone majorant) and
   k3_unc_verify.py (mpmath iv, separate code): C = 1.9999 at eps = 0.439, 1.995 at 0.45, 2.17 at 1/4, 2.057 at 3/8.
4. Lower side: exact arrangement certificates (k3_arrangement_exact.py --eps, verified by k3_arrangement_verify.py):
   sigma_3 > 2 at eps = 2/9, 1/4, 7/24. Not reached at 1/6, 1/5, 3/10, 1/3 (1/5 and 3/10 still rising with n).

## Classification
Proved / computed (partial answer to open question 6): Theorem 3.14 at k = 3 gives DHL[3,2] under GEH for eps = 2/9, 1/4,
7/24, and cannot give it for any eps in [0.439, 1/2). Heuristically the good set is an interval of about (0.19, 0.31) with
maximum about 2.003. First unresolved implication: a rigorous ceiling below 2 for small eps or for eps in [0.31, 0.439),
which must use the marginal condition. No parity-breaking content: this is about the variational shadow of the obstruction.
