**L3. CRT and orthogonality reduction to K_{uS1}, p. 73.** Proved here; Computed (C).

  phase of modulus 1.
- Modulo uS1, every c occurring in R_u is a unit: at p | S1 because c ≡ c0 and c0v0 ≡ -1 (mod p); at p | u, p ∤ S
  because cv ≡ ud0' - 1 ≡ -1 (mod p). (The condition c ≡ c0 (mod S1) alone does not make c a unit at p | u, p ∤ S.)

  c ≡ c0 (mod S1) by (1/S1)Σ_j e(j(c - c0)/S1) gives an exact identity:
