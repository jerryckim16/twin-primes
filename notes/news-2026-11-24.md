## 1. Lichtman, "Improved bounds for the binary Chowla conjecture", arXiv:2611.28392v1 (20 Nov 2026)
- PDF SHA-256 4337830d0ff830ee3952513cdc5cb77c70201ff3f54224531764bc3492b3f838. Read: pp. 1-3 and the references (p. 35-36).
- Thm 1.1 (p. 2): sum_{n<=X} mu(n)mu(n+1) <<_eps X/(log X)^{1-eps}. Thm 1.2 (p. 2): sum_{p<=X} mu(p+2) <<_eps
  pi(X)/(log X)^{1/2-eps}, "a quantitative refinement of Lü, Wang, and Wu [11] with the qualitative bound ... o(pi(X))".
  Thm 1.3: a rate for smooth shifted primes #{p <= x: P^+(p-1) <= x^{1/u}} = rho(u) pi(x) + O(pi(x)/(log x)^{1/2-eps}).
- p. 1 (quote): "Chowla's conjecture was recently resolved in the case k = 2 by OpenAI [18], and remains open for all
  k >= 3." [18] = OpenAI, "Ordinary two-point correlations of multiplicative functions", OpenAI Math Release preprint,
  Sept 24 2026 version, 87 pp. (p. 36). p. 2: OpenAI's bound is X/(log X)^c with Pilatte's exponent, c ~ 1e-5.
- p. 3: the key extra uniformity is "made available since only required one of the variables in the Type II estimate to be
  rough (c.f. Proposition 7.1 below)"; the author replaces LWW's "averaged overlapping weights" by an exact partition of
  [exp(L^alpha), exp(L^beta)] into intervals (y, y^2] (Section 2, p. 3). Cites LWW **v1** (Oct 14 version).
- AI statement (p. 3): ChatGPT-6 Astra used for writing, checking, and "transcribing routine adaptations of arguments".
- **Why it matters here.** (a) An independent expert re-derives and sharpens LWW's theorem; this is the first strong outside
  evidence that LWW's main claim holds (it is not a proof that it holds: unrefereed, AI-assisted, and built on LWW's
  framework). (b) The binary (Cesàro) Chowla conjecture is reported resolved (OpenAI, Sept 2026; we have not located or
  read that preprint). Our notes and papers that call two-point Chowla "open" are out of date if this holds
  (notes/connections.md, large-q Pellet entry; route card 2026-11-21-large-q-pellet.md item 2; the parity paper's
  wording should be checked by the paper run / weekly review; the paper is frozen, so only a 1.1.x erratum if needed).
  (c) None of this concerns Lambda(n)Lambda(n+2): mu(p+2) and mu(n)mu(n+1) are sign correlations; the parity barrier for
  twin primes (a correlation of Lambda with its own shift) is untouched.
- **Correction (Session 52, 24 Nov):** item (a) above overstates. v1 proves Thm 1.1 only; Thms 1.2-1.3 rest on "the same proof method also establishes" (p. 2), with no written proof. So v1 is evidence for the shared Type II machinery and for binary Chowla, not a written re-proof of LWW's theorem. See notes/parity-attempts/2026-11-24-lichtman-prop71-target.md.
- **Follow-up (research runs):** read Lichtman's Prop. 7.1 against our P1-P3 analysis of LWW (route cards
  2026-11-24-lww-mu-vs-lambda.md, 2026-11-24-lww-prop53-twist.md): which target properties does his Type II use? Does
  "one rough variable" change our location of the parity-breaking step (P2 => T3 small)?

## 2. Gou, Wang, Xu, "Landau's problems through ratios of L-functions", arXiv:2611.18825v1 (13 Nov 2026)
- PDF SHA-256 7734aac89774b4372a32964b10788a40ca3ded5231dd9c0e040417b12b29a79d. Read: pp. 1-4 and the AI statement.
- p. 1: "Lee [Lee26] announced a result ..., showing that a non-uniform version of the Hardy–Littlewood Prime Pair
  Conjecture can be derived from the Ratios Conjecture of Conrey–Farmer–Zirnbauer". [Lee26] = S. Lee, arXiv:2610.32672v2
  (revised 3 Nov 2026).
- Thm 1.2 (p. 3): under RH and their ratios hypothesis RC_Z(omega_Z): sum_{n<=X} Lambda(n)Lambda(n+h) = S(h)X +
  O(X^{1-omega_Z+eps}). Also conditional Goldbach, Legendre, n^2+1 (Thms 1.1, 1.3, 1.4).
- Thm 1.6 (p. 4): **unconditionally**, for a fixed odd prime power q > 10^200, sum_{F in M_n} Lambda(F)Lambda(F+h) =
  q^n S_q(h) + O_q(q^{23n/24}) uniformly in 0 != h with deg h < n. "our leading-order asymptotics are only new for
  q = p, p^2" (p. 4) — i.e. prime fields, which Sawin–Shusterman (q > 685090 p^2) do not reach. Uses Sawin's estimates for
  factorization functions in squarefree progressions and a "discriminant cohomology argument".
- AI statement (p. 6): GPT-6 Pro and "SAIR Open Math OS" used "to convert our rough ideas into rigorous proofs".
- **Why it matters here.** The Ratios Conjecture is a strong, unproved conjecture on L-function statistics (beyond RH);
  the conditional twin-prime asymptotic is consistent with the parity paper's position (parity is a limit of sieve
  methods, not of conjectural L-function inputs). It brings twin primes over Z no closer unconditionally. The function
  field result, if correct, removes Sawin–Shusterman's q > 685090 p^2 restriction for large fixed q (including q = p).
  Relevant to notes/connections.md and the parity paper's Section on S&S (frozen; for the review of 30 Nov).
