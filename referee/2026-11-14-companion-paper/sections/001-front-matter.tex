

\begin{abstract}
This is a living working paper. It does \emph{not} prove the twin prime
conjecture, and it does not improve the best known bound on small gaps between
primes. It records (i) the state of the problem as of the date above, (ii) what
this project has verified for itself, by exact computation and by the Lean~4
kernel, now including the established bound $H_1\le246$ of Polymath8b,
reproduced end to end through a certified lower bound for $M_{50,1/25}$,
(iii) small results of our own: a no-go statement for a natural
attempt to sharpen the pigeonhole step, on the plain and on the
$\varepsilon$-enlarged simplex; a generating-function formula that makes
certified computation with all symmetric test functions cheap; an upper
bound showing that the $\varepsilon$-enlargement of Polymath8b cannot, by
itself, reach any $k\le29$; numerical evidence that this upper bound is close
to the limit of every dual weight depending only on $t_m$ and $t_1+\dots+t_k$,
and heuristic evidence that the $\varepsilon$-enlarged simplex stops at
$k=50$; and, for the one-band hybrid supports of Stadlmann, a rigorous
floor (no $k\le28$ at his level of distribution, whatever the caps), and an
exact formula for his capped functionals, which gives a certified capped
ratio $kJ_T/I_T=4.0120>4$ at $k=49$, computed in exact arithmetic by two
independent programs: the variational step of Stadlmann's $H_1\le240$ is
thereby reproduced, while his new equidistribution estimates, on which $240$
rests, have not been verified by us; the same computation confirms the
variational criterion of Charton et al.\ at $k=45$ ($4.0027>4$) for the caps
of their Table~3 but not, with our test function, for the different caps of
their Lemma~9.1 ($3.9737$), and with those caps no polynomial in our
$846$-dimensional space reaches $3.99$ (a ceiling certified in ball
arithmetic, with weights checked exactly at a random point); optimising the test function on Stadlmann's
capped support itself, with exact Gram matrices obtained by running the exact
evaluator backwards, raises his margin only from $4.0120$ to $4.0124$;
for the support of Kintali at $k=48$ (claimed $H_1\le236$), our own test
function of degree~$22$ gives $4.0061>4$ (two independent exact programs since version~0.12), and
for the published witness of Song and Yue (same support) we recompute their
capped numerator exactly: it equals their reference integer digit for digit,
as does their cap-free denominator, so their criterion $48J/I_0>2001/2000$
holds; since version~0.11 this is confirmed by a second, independent exact
program, so the variational step of $H_1\le236$ is reproduced (its analytic
input is not verified by us, and the project's bound $236$ is conditional on
it); version~0.12 adds an elementary observation (Bonferroni) that lets a
one-sided certificate skip the most expensive inclusion--exclusion pieces
(Proposition~\ref{prop:bonf}); and
(iv) a programme of original
directions that later versions will test. Every statement carries one of the
labels \lbl{Lean-checked}, \lbl{Computed}, \lbl{Proved here},
\lbl{Literature}, \lbl{Claimed} (a preprint not yet independently
verified), or \lbl{Open}.
\end{abstract}
